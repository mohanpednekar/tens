Tens — Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026. Applies to the Tens web game (mohanpednekar.github.io/tens) and the Tens apps for Android and iOS.

Tens is an incremental game made by an independent developer ("we"). The game works without an account and without sending anything to us. This policy explains the few optional features that do involve other services.

1. Data stored on your device

Your game progress, save slots and settings (theme, Buy max mode) are stored locally in your browser or app storage. They never leave your device unless you use cloud save. You can erase them at any time in Settings → Danger zone, or by clearing site data / uninstalling the app.

2. Cloud save (optional account)

If you create a cloud save account, we use Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (provided by Google LLC) to:

  • store your email address, a hashed password and an account ID, to sign you in;
  • store a copy of your game save (game progress only), the time it was uploaded and the platform it came from, so you can restore it on another device.

Firebase also processes your IP address and basic device information as part of providing and securing the service. Data is stored on Google Cloud servers and is accessible only to your account. We do not use it for advertising and do not sell it. See Firebase privacy and security.

3. Advertising (apps only, when enabled)

The Android and iOS apps may offer optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. Ads are never shown unless you choose to watch one. When ads are enabled, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. In regions where consent is required we ask for it through Google's consent form, and on iOS we ask for permission before any tracking (App Tracking Transparency). You can change your choice in your device settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. The web game shows no ads.

4. In-app purchases (apps only, when enabled)

Optional purchases (such as "Remove ads" or a tip) are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to validate purchases and restore them on your devices. RevenueCat receives purchase records (product, time, price, store transaction ID) together with a random app user ID. See the RevenueCat privacy policy. No game content requires payment.

5. What we do not do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, no social logins, no contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • We do not sell personal data or share it with data brokers.

7. Deleting your account and data

In the game, open Settings → Cloud save → Delete account and data and confirm with your password. This immediately and permanently deletes your Firebase account and your cloud save. If you cannot access the app, open a request at github.com/mohanpednekar/tens/issues with the email address of the account (do not post your password); we will delete the account and its data within 30 days and confirm. Local saves are deleted with the app or via Settings → Danger zone.

Purchase records kept by Google, Apple and RevenueCat are retained under their own policies and tax obligations.

8. Children

Tens is not directed at children under 13, and cloud save accounts are not intended for them. Ads, when enabled, are requested as non-personalized where required.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law) you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your data and to object to processing. Contact us using the link above to exercise them.

10. Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date.
